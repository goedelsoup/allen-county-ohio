**Why this was not found before, and what the corpus had wrong.**
[The 1920 entry](ohio-election-statistics-1920.md) recorded that archive.org held *Ohio Election
Statistics* for 1895, 1901, 1905 and 1920 and that "the rest of the run has not been located". Both
halves are wrong. Three of those four items are **bound runs of several years** rather than single
volumes — the one catalogued as "1905" is 2,009 pages and carries the statistics for 1898, 1903,
1904, 1905 and 1908 — and before the separate title existed the election returns were a division of
the Secretary of State's *Annual Report*, of which archive.org holds 1890/91, 1892/93, 1910/11 and
1930. A search on the title could not have found them, because the title is *Annual Report*.
[verified] — the six items above.

**Where a presidential return lives is not the year of the election.** The certified abstract is
printed in the report for the election year, but the office reprints the same county figures in the
volume for the following odd year, beside that year's governor, as a comparative table — and again
in comparative tables years later. So the volume that answers "1892" is the report for 1893 and the
volume that answers "1908" is the report for 1910. This is
[ask the volume just after the gap](../decisions/ask-the-volume-just-after-the-gap.yml) in a
different series, and it is why four of the six elections here came out of volumes published two,
four or twelve years after the vote.

**Every table read here checks itself, and two of them check the whole state.** The 1888 abstract
prints a plurality column that is the difference of its two leading columns; Allen County's printed
Democratic plurality of 1,362 is exactly 5,123 less 3,761. Its state totals — 416,054, 396,455,
24,356, 3,496 — close on a printed plurality of 19,599. The 1892 table's state row prints 405,187
and 404,115 against a plurality of 1,072, and the 1893 governor's row 433,342 and 352,347 against
80,995. A misread digit in any of the four breaks an identity on the same page. [verified]

**The same county figure is printed more than once and the printings are not always equal.** Allen
County's 1896 Republican vote is 4,959 in the 1896 abstract and in the 1904 volume's comparative
table, and 4,956 in the 1908 volume's. See
[a reprint is not a second witness](../decisions/a-reprint-is-not-a-second-witness.yml).

**Two OCR failures, in opposite directions.** The Internet Archive scans emit these tables
row-major, which makes a county's line readable in the text layer — and misread it: the 1892 table's
text gives Allen 3,079 and 4,946 where the page prints 3,579 and 4,945. Google's scans of the same
series emit the tables column-major, county names in one run and figures in another, so a row cannot
be reassembled at all. Nothing here was taken from a text layer; the text layer was used to find
pages and the figures were read from renders. [verified]

**What it will not answer.** No precincts and no townships: the county is one row in every table
above, as it is in [the 1920 volume](ohio-election-statistics-1920.md). And the run stops before it
finishes — archive.org holds no Ohio volume covering the general elections of 1912 or 1916, so those
two are not here and this catalog names no shelf that has them.
