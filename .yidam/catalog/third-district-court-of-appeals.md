**Its office is in Allen County**, at 204 N. Main Street in Lima — the same street as the county
prosecutor. [verified] So a court serving seventeen counties is seated in this one, and its records
for this district are kept by [the county's clerk of courts](../corpus/office/allen-county-clerk-of-courts.yml).

**The seventeen counties:** Allen, Auglaize, Crawford, Defiance, Hancock, Hardin, Henry, Logan,
Marion, Mercer, Paulding, Putnam, Seneca, Shelby, Union, Van Wert and Wyandot. [verified] Counted
here: seventeen names, matching the count the court itself gives. [inference]

**Four judges sit**, each first elected in a different year — John R. Willamowski (2006, re-elected
to a fourth term in 2024), William R. Zimmerman (2016), Mark C. Miller (2020, to a vacancy left by
Judge Vernon Preston's retirement) and Juergen A. Waldick (2022, to the seat of retired Judge
Stephen R. Shaw). [verified]

**They are not Allen County officers and this corpus does not give them nodes.** They are elected by
the electors of all seventeen counties, so they fall outside the line this corpus draws for a person
node — see [the courts](../corpus/jurisdiction/third-district-court-of-appeals.yml). Their names are
recorded here, in the catalog, where a reader who needs them will find them.
