**It is thirty years newer than the newest narrative source this corpus held.** Nine nodes here
say some version of *the corpus's newest narrative source is the 1921 history*, and for the city of
Lima that is no longer true. [verified] — the booklet's imprint, "Published by Lima Association of
Commerce - February, 1952". The gap it closes is 1921 to 1951 and it closes it thinly: a paragraph
a decade, with dates and without figures.

**It was written to win an argument.** The booklet's second half is a petition — "It is for the
above reasons that we believe that it is imperative that Allen County be included in the Anthony
Wayne Parkway" — addressed to a state committee then deciding which counties a memorial highway
would run through. [verified] — the same text. The history is the case, and the case wants the
county to look consequential; see
[a parcel is not an installation](../decisions/a-parcel-is-not-an-installation.yml) for the rule
this corpus applies to a document with an interest.

**Its publisher is a chamber of commerce, and its industrial passages read like one.** "Lima now
ranks ninth in the state in volume of business transacted"; the city is "a city of homes, of
diversified industry, of modern well-equipped municipal services, of prosperous retail stores".
[verified] — the same text. Dates and institution names are taken from it here; superlatives and
rankings are not.

**What it is used for.** Dated civic changes the corpus could not otherwise fix: the return to
mayor-and-council in 1933, the buses that replaced the electric street cars in 1938, the airport of
1927 and the airport of 1935, the post office of 1931, the county's centennial celebration in the
same year, the stadium and the radio station of 1936, and the Westinghouse small-motors division
that took over the idle Garford and Relay Motor Company building in that year. [verified] — the
same text.

**And one disagreement with the 1921 history, which is worth more than the agreements.** Hackman
puts the electrification of Lima's street railway on 4 July 1887; the 1921 history has the first
electric car in 1886. [verified] — the same text, against
[A Standard History of Allen County, 1921](rusler-allen-county-1921.md). Two local historians a
generation apart, one year apart, and nothing here decides between them.
