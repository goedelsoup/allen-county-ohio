# site

A site is a fixed work of human construction and the ground it occupies — a refinery, a
locomotive works, a tank plant, a canal, a courthouse, a quarry, a lock. Nodes here answer
*what was built here, and is it still standing*.

A site is separate from the [organization](../organization/) that runs it because built works
outlast their operators and are sold between them: the refinery at Lima has had several
owners and is one site throughout. It is separate from [natural-feature](../natural-feature/)
on the built/unbuilt line, which is the distinction that decides whether a thing has an
operator at all. Linear works — a canal, a rail line — are sites too; they simply have an
extent rather than a point.
