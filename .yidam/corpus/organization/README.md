# organization

An organization is a named collective actor that is not a unit of government — a company, a
college, a newspaper, a congregation, an association, a union. Nodes here answer *what body
did this*.

The line against [jurisdiction](../jurisdiction/) is exactly one thing: whether the body
holds governmental authority over territory. A sheriff's office is an organization within a
jurisdiction; the county is the jurisdiction. The line against [site](../site/) is that an
organization acts and a site is built — a company can move, close, merge and be renamed
while the works it operated stand unchanged.
