# place

A place is a named area of ground that people in Allen County refer to by its name — the
county itself, one of its twelve townships as territory, the city of Lima, a village, an
unincorporated crossroads. Nodes here answer *where*, and they are deliberately not the
government of the same name: Lima the place and the City of Lima the corporation have
different birthdays and different extents, and the corpus keeps them apart so that an
annexation can move one without silently moving the other.

Most place nodes carry a `centroid` and `area_sq_mi` taken from the Census Bureau's
published gazetteer, which makes them the anchor other classes hang location from. Two
places in this county — Bluffton and Delphos — straddle a county line, and their nodes say
so, because a figure reported for "Allen County, Bluffton" is a part and not a total.
