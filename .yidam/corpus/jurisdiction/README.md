# jurisdiction

A jurisdiction is a unit of government holding authority over defined territory — Allen
County itself, one of its townships, an incorporated municipality, a school district. Nodes
here answer *who governs*, and they are separate from the [place](../place/) of the same
name because a government has a creation instrument, officers, a budget and a boundary that
annexation moves, none of which are true of the ground or its name.

Several jurisdictions cover the same ground at once and change independently: a village, the
township around it, the county, and a school district may all have authority over one acre,
with four different boundaries and four different histories. That overlap is the reason this
class exists rather than being a property of place.
