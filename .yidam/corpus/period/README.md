# period

A period is a named span this corpus interprets as a unit — the canal era, the oil boom,
deindustrialization. Nodes here answer *what kind of time was this*.

A period is not a long event, and the difference is not duration. An event has a date and
participants and either happened or did not. A period has bounds the corpus chose, a
character it asserts, and is the thing other happenings are situated in. That makes a period
node the most interpretive kind of node in the corpus: its start and end dates are an
argument, and `boundary_basis` exists so that the argument is stated rather than implied by
a bare pair of years.
