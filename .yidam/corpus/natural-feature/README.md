# natural-feature

A natural feature is a physical feature of the land or water that nobody built — a river, a
creek, a watershed, a glacial landform, a bedrock formation, an aquifer. Nodes here answer
*what is the ground itself doing*.

For Allen County the water is the part that carries the most explanatory weight. The county
sits in the Lake Erie drainage: its streams run north and west to the Auglaize and thence to
the Maumee, which is why its nineteenth-century transport problem was solved by a canal and
why its northern townships were part of the Great Black Swamp until they were drained. A
node here is worth writing when something else in the corpus is sited, drained, or bounded
by it.
