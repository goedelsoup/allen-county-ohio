# office

An office is a named position of authority that persists across the people who hold it —
Sheriff of Allen County, Mayor of Lima, Judge of the Court of Common Pleas. Nodes here
answer *what position exists, and on what terms*.

An office is a role and not a person: it has a creation date, a selection method, a term
length, and sometimes several seats at once. Who occupies it is a [tenure](../tenure/). The
separation is what lets the corpus answer questions about the office across two centuries
without any single person's node growing to hold them.
