# event

An event is a bounded happening at a stated time — a treaty signing, an act of erection, an
oil strike, a plant closing, an election, a flood. Nodes here answer *what happened, and
when*.

Events are the corpus's temporal spine. The choice to build time out of happenings rather
than out of dates attached to persistent things is what lets the county's origin be modeled
honestly: the acts that created Allen County largely occurred outside it, which is why the
`affected` edge exists alongside `occurred-in`.
