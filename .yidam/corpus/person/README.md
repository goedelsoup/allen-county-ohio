# person

A person node is a named individual human being. Nodes here answer *who*.

The class is deliberately thin on what a person *was* in any official sense. A person does
not stop being one by leaving office, so the offices they held and the years they held them
are not properties here — they are [tenure](../tenure/) nodes, which is the whole reason
that class exists. What belongs on a person is what is true of them across their life:
dates, birthplace, what they were principally known for.

This is the class where invention is most tempting and most damaging, because a fabricated
biographical detail is well-formed, plausible, and indistinguishable from a sourced one. Tag
every non-obvious claim.
