# corpus

This corpus covers Allen County, Ohio — its places, jurisdictions, reporting divisions,
built works and natural features; the people, organizations and offices that have acted in
it; and the events and periods that structure its history from pre-statehood occupation
through the present. The level of analysis is the individual named thing: one township, one
plant, one office, one happening — not the county in aggregate, which is what the edges
between those nodes are for.

Each file is one node. Nodes are small and focused — one concept, one relation, one artifact,
or one open question. Every node must have at least one outgoing link.

See [corpus conventions](../.vendor/prelude/guidelines/directories.md#corpus) for authoring rules,
the authored/generated node distinction, and `[verified]`/`[inference]`/`[open]` claim tags.
