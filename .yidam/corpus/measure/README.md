# measure

A measure is a quantity recorded for a named subject at a stated time, carrying the source it
came from. Nodes here answer *how many, and as of when*.

Numbers are nodes in this corpus rather than properties of the things they describe, because
a figure without its date, its unit and its provenance is not checkable — and pasting numbers
onto the node they describe is the ordinary way a citation gets lost. Every measure here
cites a [catalog](../../catalog/) entry.

The class carries a specific hazard this county makes concrete: an *estimate* and a *count*
sit in the same source file, and two figures for the same place may describe different
ground if a boundary moved between them. `method` is where that gets said.
